桌子状态TableStatus 和 玩家状态UserGameStatus 约束了玩家的动作

-----

* TableStatus 约束

  |             | kFree         | kInProgress | kXjEnd        | kDjEnd |
  |-------------+---------------+-------------+---------------+--------|
  | Enter       | ✅            | 允许旁观 ✅ | ✅            | ❌     |
  | Sit         | 有空闲椅子 ✅ | ❌          | 有空闲椅子 ✅ | ❌     |
  | StandUp     | ✅            | ❌          | ✅            | ❌     |
  | Leave       | ✅            | ❌          | ✅            | ❌     |
  | Ready       | ✅            | ❌          | ✅            | ❌     |
  | CancelReady | ✅            | ❌          | ✅            | ❌     |
  | Host        | ❌            | ✅          | ❌            | ❌     |
  | CancelHost  | ✅            | ✅          | ✅            | ❌     |

-----

* UserGameStatus 约束

    |             | kNotInTable | kSit | kStandUp | kReady | kPlaying |
    |-------------+-------------+------+----------+--------+----------|
    | Enter       | ✅          | ❌   | ❌       | ❌     | ❌       |
    | Sit         | ✅          | ❌   | ✅       | ❌     | ❌       |
    | StandUp     | ❌          | ✅   | ❌       | ❌     | ❌       |
    | Leave       | ❌          | ✅   | ✅       | ✅     | ❌       |
    | Ready       | ❌          | ✅   | ❌       | ❌     | ❌       |
    | CancelReady | ❌          | ❌   | ❌       | ✅     | ❌       |
    | Host        | ❌          | ❌   | ❌       | ❌     | ✅       |
    | CancelHost  | ✅          | ✅   | ✅       | ✅     | ✅       |

    -----

* Action Host/CancelHost

  玩家托管状态 IsHost 是一个独立的status, 不在UserGameStatus 中.
  玩家Action Host/CancelHost 需要加上IsHost 的判断

  |            | 未托管 | 已托管 |
  |------------+--------+--------|
  | Host       | ✅     | ❌     |
  | CancelHost | ❌     | ✅     |
