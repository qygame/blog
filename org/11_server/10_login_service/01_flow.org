* 认证流程
  #+begin_src mermaid :file result.png
    sequenceDiagram
        participant Client
        participant LB as LB集群
        participant LoginService
        participant GateService
        participant ActorService
        participant Redis

        Client->>LB: 账号、密码
        LB->>LoginService: 转发
        LoginService->>LoginService: 查询数据库,校验登陆权限
        LoginService->>Redis: 获取GateService列表及负载
        Redis-->>LoginService: 返回GateService列表
        LoginService->>LoginService: 选择GateService, 生成Token
        LoginService->>Redis: 保存Token与登陆上下文
        LoginService-->>LB: 返回GateService Ip、Port以及Token
        LB-->>Client: 转发

        Client->>GateService: Token
        GateService->>Redis: 校验Token
        Redis-->>GateService: 返回Token对应的上下文
        GateService->>GateService: 记录map(uid, session)
        GateService->>ActorService:  角色登陆(uid)
        ActorService->>ActorService: 创建User
        ActorService-->>GateService: 返回用户数据
        GateService-->>Client: 转发
  #+end_src


* 路由请求
  1. Client -> LB集群: 通过DNS 或Anycast 将客户端请求引导至LB
  2. LB集群 -> LoginService: LB通过轮询在LoginService 集群中选择实例
  3. LoginService 返回GateService信息: GateService集群定期向Redis上报自身状态. LoginService根据策略在集群中选择合适的GateService
  4. GateService -> ActorService: GateService根据uid 通过一致性Hash 选择ActorService
