#+title: 创建/加入房间

* 流程
  #+begin_src mermaid :file result.png
    sequenceDiagram
        participant Client
        participant GateService
        participant ActorService
        participant RoomManagerService
        participant RoomService

        Client->>GateService: 创建房间(data)
        GateService->>ActorService: 转发
        ActorService->>ActorService: 校验用户状态
        ActorService->>RoomManagerService: 用户创建房间(data, user)
        RoomManagerService ->> RoomManagerService: 选择RoomService,校验RoomService状态
        RoomManagerService ->> RoomService: 用户创建房间(data, user)
        RoomService ->>RoomService: 创建(选择)Table,玩家加入Table
        RoomService -->> RoomManagerService: 返回结果
        RoomManagerService -->> ActorService: 返回结果给client
        ActorService -->> GateService: 转发

        Client ->> GateService: 向RoomService请求Table数据&&玩家数据
  #+end_src

  -----

  #+attr_shortcode: info
  #+begin_hint
  Client 创建/加入成功房间后, 主动去RoomService拉取房间数据&&玩家数据, 完成子游戏场景的初始化
  后续数据的更新(房间数据 && 玩家数据) 会由RoomService 主动推送
  #+end_hint
